# 🧪 KnQ Reels: Privy & Polygon Crypto Workflow Testing Guide

This guide outlines step-by-step instructions to verify, test, and audit the newly implemented **Privy.io Embedded Wallet** flow and **Platform-Sponsored Gasless NFT Claiming** workflow on Polygon (Amoy Testnet).

---

## 🏗️ Prerequisites for Testing

Ensure both your frontend and backend environments are running locally:
*   **Frontend**: `http://localhost:5173` (configured with `VITE_PRIVY_APP_ID=cmpl0yuqc000s0dl7e5f1jfjk`)
*   **Backend**: `http://localhost:5001` or dynamic port (configured with `PRIVY_APP_ID` & `PRIVY_APP_SECRET`)
*   **Web3 Integration**: `ENABLE_WEB3=true` in `backend/.env` with `MINTER_PRIVATE_KEY` funded with some native **Amoy POL/MATIC** (for paying deployment/sponsor transaction fees).

---

## 🕹️ Test Case 1: Social Login & Embedded Wallet Auto-Creation (New User)

### Goal
Verify that logging in via Google OAuth or Email OTP automatically creates a secure embedded wallet for new users and registers them in the database.

### Steps
1.  Navigate to the sign-in page: `http://localhost:5173/signin`.
2.  Click the primary yellow button **"Continue with Google / OTP"**.
3.  Choose either:
    *   **Google OAuth**: Log in using your Google credentials.
    *   **Email OTP**: Enter an email address, fetch the 6-digit OTP from your inbox, and verify.
4.  Once Privy completes the flow, it will redirect you to the Home Feed `/home`.
5.  **Audit Step (Database)**:
    *   Open MongoDB Compass or Atlas.
    *   Locate the `users` collection.
    *   Search for your email.
    *   **Verify** that the document has:
        *   `walletAddress`: A lowercase Polygon address generated by Privy (e.g., `0x839d...`).
        *   `role`: `"User"`.

## 🕹️ Test Case 2: Interactive Wallet Onboarding (Existing Web2 User)

### Goal
Verify that existing users who originally signed up using only Email/Password are *not* automatically forced to generate a wallet, but can link one interactively with clear consent.

### Steps
1.  Register a new account manually via `http://localhost:5173/signup` using a standard Email and Password.
2.  Log in using these traditional credentials at `http://localhost:5173/signin`.
3.  Once redirected to the Home Feed `/home`, observe the top of the dashboard.
4.  **Verify** that a premium, subtle gold banner appears:
    > 📁 *“Enable your secure digital wallet to collect and trade creator collectibles [Enable Wallet]”*
5.  Click the **"Enable Wallet"** button.
6.  Complete the Privy wallet linking dialog (links your active email to Privy to generate the secure embedded keys).
7.  Once finished, the page automatically updates:
    *   The onboarding banner disappears.
    *   **Verify** in your database that the `walletAddress` field for this existing user is now populated with their newly linked Privy wallet address.

## 🕹️ Test Case 3: Sponsored Gasless NFT Claiming (Winner Experience)

### Goal
Verify that the auction winner can claim their won NFT collectibles completely gasless, without managing MATIC/POL or seeing MetaMask popups.

### Setup (Admin Tasks)
To test a claim, we must have a won auction that is minted to the Vault:
1.  **Mint the NFT**:
    *   As an Admin (using postman/curl or admin panel), ensure the auction status is `ended` and has a valid `winner` linked.
    *   Call `POST /api/nft/admin/prepare/:auctionId` to pin media to IPFS.
    *   Call `POST /api/nft/admin/mint/:auctionId` to mint the NFT on-chain to the Vault contract (`VAULT_CONTRACT_ADDRESS`).
    *   Verify the auction status has updated to `minted` on the database.

### Winner Actions (Claiming)
1.  Log in as the **Winner** of this auction.
2.  Navigate directly to the claim page: `http://localhost:5173/nfts/claim/<auctionId>`.
3.  Observe the breakdown:
    *   **Verify** it states: `"Sponsored Asset Price"` and `"Sponsored by Platform"`.
4.  If your secure wallet isn't active yet, click the gold button: **"Enable Secure Digital Wallet"** to activate it.
5.  Once active, click the gold **"Claim Sponsored NFT"** action.
6.  Observe the loading state:
    *   *"Processing Sponsored Settlement... Submitting on-chain transaction. This will be instant and gasless for you!"*
7.  Upon confirmation:
    *   The success panel appears: *"Asset Claimed Successfully!"*
    *   Click **"View on Polygonscan"** to audit the transaction.
8.  **Audit Step (On-Chain & Database)**:
    *   Check Polygonscan to verify that the gas fees were fully sponsored and paid by the platform's minter wallet.
    *   Verify the auction's `nftStatus` on the database has updated to `deposit_received`.
    *   Verify that your user now owns the NFT inside `/my-collection`.

## 🔍 Troubleshooting & Verification Checklist

| Symptom | Cause | Resolution |
|---|---|---|
| `Privy keys are not configured on server` | Missing `.env` variables | Double-check that `PRIVY_APP_ID` and `PRIVY_APP_SECRET` are declared in `backend/.env`. |
| `Axios 401 Unauthorized` | Missing Auth Headers | Ensure the custom JWT token in Zustand store is loaded and set as `Bearer <token>` inside axios requests. |
| Minter transaction reverts | Sponsor Wallet has no POL | The server's `MINTER_PRIVATE_KEY` wallet is responsible for submitting transactions. Ensure it holds at least **0.5 POL** on Polygon Amoy. |
